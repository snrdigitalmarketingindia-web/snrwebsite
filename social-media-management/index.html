SNR Digital Marketing provides full social media account management for businesses across India. We access your Facebook Pages and Instagram Business accounts through Meta's official permission system — meaning you stay in complete control at all times. Our team handles content creation, scheduling, and publishing while you focus on running your business. Facebook Page Management

Instagram Business Account Management

We manage content for your Instagram Business account — feed posts, stories, and reels — published on your behalf with your explicit authorisation. We never access personal Instagram profiles.

Shared Publishing Calendar

Every client receives access to a shared content calendar showing all planned posts before they are scheduled. Nothing goes live without your approval, and you can view the full history of published content at any time.

How We Work

1

Account Authorisation

You connect your Facebook Page and/or Instagram Business account through Meta's official permission flow. This gives us the specific access needed to publish on your behalf — no more, no less.

2

Content Planning

We build a monthly content calendar covering themes, post types, and publishing times. The calendar is shared with you for review and approval before anything is scheduled.

3

Content Creation

Our team creates graphics, captions, and short video scripts for each approved post, following your brand guidelines and the content plan you approved.

4

Scheduling & Publishing

Once content is approved, we schedule posts using the agreed calendar and publish to your connected accounts at optimal times for your audience.

5

Report & Review

At month end we share a full performance report — every post published, its reach and engagement, and recommendations for next month.
